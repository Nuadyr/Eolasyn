    About Eolasyn

    Eolasyn gathers moveset, raid boss, Team Rocket and PvP information from public community sites and links to the source on every entry. PvE attack, PvE defence and PvP lists are worked out from the Pokédex when you open them. Raid bosses and Team Rocket lineups are fetched live from the first source that answers. Team Rocket lineups come first from ScrapedDuck, which reads them from LeekDuck.com. Some Team Rocket sources block direct reads, so those pages are fetched through a public relay (allorigins.win), which can see the address requested. Rankings change with balance updates and events, so check the source before you spend Stardust or Candy.

    How to read the numbers: DPS is damage per second against a neutral target. TDO is total damage output before the Pokémon faints. Both depend on level, weather and each site's simulation, so compare numbers only within one source.

    Entries you add or import, and the Pokémon you mark as caught, stay in your browser. Nothing leaves your device unless you export a file or open a source link. This is a fan project and is not affiliated with Niantic, Nintendo or The Pokémon Company.
